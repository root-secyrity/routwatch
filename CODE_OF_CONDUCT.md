# Code of conduct

Be civil. This is one person's tool with a public issue tracker.

- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- No insults, harassment, threats or bigotry.
- No spam or advertising, and no issues demanding free support.
- Assume the other side's router behaves differently from yours.

Report unacceptable behaviour through **Security -> Report a vulnerability** on
this repository - reports are private and only the maintainer reads them.
A first breach gets a warning; a repeat or a threat ends the discussion and the
account is blocked from the tracker. Anything that breaks these rules may be
edited, closed or deleted without notice.
